Execution Protocols

01. Discovery & Alignment

Understanding the client’s goals, audience, and brand direction to ensure clarity on scope, expectations, and success before creative work begins. Deep analysis of brand DNA and market positioning to identify "The Friction Point."

02. Concept Development

Ideas and visual directions are developed from insights gathered, defining the creative approach, storytelling style, and overall look for client approval.

03. Pre-Production

All concepts are finalized into clear plans, with assets, layouts, scripts, and schedules prepared to ensure a smooth production process.

04. Production

Approved concepts are executed through design, video, animation, or interactive media, with continuous internal quality checks.

05. Review & Iteration

Client feedback is gathered and applied through structured revision rounds to refine the work while maintaining creative intent.

06. Finalization & Delivery

The project is polished, quality-checked, and delivered in the required formats, ready for immediate use.

07. Deployment Support

Support is provided for publishing or rollout to ensure the content performs effectively across platforms.

08. Post-Project Review & Archiving

Projects are reviewed for improvement, archived securely, and client feedback is documented for future reference.
